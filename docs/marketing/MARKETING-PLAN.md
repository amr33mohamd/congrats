**Tagline (EN):** Send a moment they'll never forget.

## 1. Executive Summary

Congrats (مبروك) is a bilingual, mobile-first web platform that lets anyone build a personalized, animated "congratulations experience" in minutes and share it through a single private link — purpose-built for how Egyptians actually celebrate: over WhatsApp, in family group chats, and on Instagram and TikTok.

The core insight: greeting cards are dead, but *moments* are not. Egyptians spend heavily on occasions — Eid, weddings, engagements, graduations, newborns — yet digital greetings remain stuck at static images, forwarded "صباح الخير" graphics, and generic e-cards that nobody saves. Congrats turns a congratulation into a small, delightful, step-by-step animated experience with the recipient's name, photos, custom messages, and music, ending in a moment worth screenshotting and re-sharing.

**Why Congrats wins:**

- **Local-first, not localized-later.** Egyptian colloquial Arabic, RTL-correct design, EGP pricing, and InstaPay payments — not a translated foreign template tool.
- **Built-in distribution.** Every experience is a shareable link with a QR code. Every share is a free ad. Recipients become the next senders. This is the viral loop the business is designed around.
- **Emotion at low price.** A free tier removes friction; Premium (~49 EGP+) adds animation and music — an impulse-priced upgrade for moments people care about.

**90-day goal:** Launch publicly, validate the viral loop, and reach an early base of paying senders while proving a K-factor approaching or exceeding 1.0.

**Headline first-90-day targets:**

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Experiences created | 20,000 |
| Unique senders | 12,000 |
| Paid conversions | 1,800 (≈15% of senders) |
| K-factor (viral coefficient) | ≥ 0.8, trending to 1.0 |
| Blended CAC (paid blended) | ≤ 35 EGP |
| Gross revenue | ≈ 110,000 EGP |

## 2. Product & Value Proposition

### What it is
A web app where a user: (1) picks an occasion template, (2) personalizes it — recipient name, custom messages, photo uploads, animations, music, (3) previews it live, (4) pays, and (5) shares a private link + QR code. Eight occasion categories: **Anniversary, Valentine's, Proposal, Eid, Birthday, Graduation, Newborn, Wedding.**

### Jobs-to-be-done (JTBD)

| When I... | I want to... | So that... |
|---|---|---|
| have a friend graduating | send something that feels personal, not a forwarded image | they feel genuinely seen and celebrated |
| can't be physically present (expat, traveling, busy) | deliver a "moment" remotely | distance doesn't make my wish feel cheap |
| want to propose / mark an anniversary | create something romantic and surprising on a budget | I get the emotional payoff without a big production |
| celebrate Eid with a large family/friend list | send something warm at scale, fast | I stay connected without 50 identical copy-pastes |
| want to impress on social | make something screenshot-worthy and re-shareable | the moment lives beyond the chat |

### Core value proposition
> **Congrats turns a 30-second congratulation into a moment people screenshot, save, and re-share — personalized, animated, and ready to send on WhatsApp in minutes, for the price of a coffee.**

### Why now
- **WhatsApp is the default emotional channel** in Egypt; family and friend group chats are where occasions are celebrated.
- **Static greetings have peaked.** Forwarded morning/holiday graphics are background noise; people crave personalization.
- **Creator-tool literacy is high** thanks to Canva, CapCut, and TikTok — users now *expect* to personalize, but lack a fast, occasion-specific tool.
- **InstaPay adoption** has made instant low-value digital payments culturally normal, removing the historical friction of paying small amounts online in Egypt.
- **Animation as a differentiator** is finally cheap to ship (modern web + Remotion-style motion), so a small team can deliver premium-feeling motion at scale.

## 3. Market Analysis

### Egypt digital & social landscape
Egypt is one of the largest internet and social-media markets in the Arab world, with a young, mobile-first, highly social population. Key dynamics relevant to Congrats:

- **Mobile-first by default.** The overwhelming majority of social and messaging usage is on smartphones — the product's mobile-first, RTL-safe design is correctly prioritized.
- **WhatsApp is near-universal** as the primary personal messaging app and the de-facto channel for sharing links, photos, and occasion wishes. This is the single most important distribution surface for Congrats.
- **Instagram and TikTok** drive culture and discovery among the 16–34 core demographic; short vertical video (Reels/TikTok) is the dominant content format.
- **Facebook & Facebook Groups** remain heavily used across a broad age range and are strong for community/interest-group seeding (university groups, neighborhood groups, "buy & sell," wedding-planning communities).

### Gifting & occasion spending
Egyptian social life is occasion-dense. Spending and gifting cluster around:

- **Eid al-Fitr & Eid al-Adha** — the two biggest gifting and greeting moments of the year (Eidiya, family visits, mass well-wishing).
- **Wedding & engagement season** — large, recurring, socially central; many weddings cluster in cooler months and around summer.
- **Graduation season** — late spring / early summer; high emotional and social-sharing intensity among youth.
- **Valentine's Day (and Egypt's local "Sham El-Nessim"/spring sentiment)** — strong among young couples and students.
- **Newborns & birthdays** — steady, year-round baseline demand.

### Seasonality map (demand calendar)

| Period | Peak occasion(s) | Demand signal |
|---|---|---|
| Jan–Feb | **Valentine's** | High romantic spike (couples, students) |
| Mar–Apr | **Eid al-Fitr** (Ramadan-dependent), spring | Very high — mass greetings |
| May–Jul | **Graduation**, **Wedding** season ramp | Very high — youth + family |
| Jun–Aug | **Eid al-Adha**, summer weddings | High |
| Year-round | **Birthday, Newborn, Anniversary, Proposal** | Steady baseline |

> Ramadan/Eid dates shift ~10–11 days earlier each year; the calendar above must be re-anchored to the Hijri calendar annually.

### Competitors & alternatives

| Alternative | What they offer | Where Congrats wins |
|---|---|---|
| **Generic e-cards** (123Greetings-style, WhatsApp forwards) | Static, impersonal, free | Animated, personalized, name + photos + music; a "moment," not a graphic |
| **Canva / templates** | Powerful design, but generic and effortful; not occasion-flow | Zero design skill, occasion-specific step-by-step flow, Egyptian Arabic, shareable link + QR |
| **CapCut / video editing** | Custom video possible | Requires editing skill and time; Congrats is a 5-minute guided flow |
| **Physical gifts & cards** | Tangible | Instant, remote-friendly, low-cost, no logistics; complements rather than competes |
| **Instagram/TikTok DIY posts** | Public, casual | Private, personal, structured "experience" with an emotional arc |
| **Florists / gift shops with cards** | Local, in-person | Digital reach, expat-friendly, scalable to many recipients |

**Competitive whitespace:** No dominant player owns *occasion-specific, animated, Egyptian-Arabic, link-shareable congratulations*. The gap is real and defensible through local craft (language, motion, payment, taste).

## 6. Brand & Visual Identity Guidance

### Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Rose | `#F0436E` | Primary CTAs, key accents, love/celebration energy |
| Deep accent | Deep rose | `#AE1F44` | Hover/active states, depth, romantic occasions |
| Premium | Gold | `#D6A435` | Premium tier, "shine" moments, finishing flourishes |
| Text | Ink | `#14100E` | Body copy, high-contrast text |
| Surface | Cream | `#FAF8F7` | Backgrounds, breathing room, warmth |

### Usage principles
- **Rose leads, gold accents.** Rose is the brand's heartbeat; gold signals premium and the "wow" climax — use gold sparingly so it stays special (and aligns with the Premium tier).
- **Cream over white.** Always favor `#FAF8F7` surfaces for a warmer, gift-like feel.
- **Ink for legibility**, never pure black.
- **Gradients:** rose → deep rose for emotional surfaces; add gold sparkle only at climactic moments (reveals, endings).

### Logo & lockups
- Maintain clear space equal to the height of the wordmark's main letter.
- Provide AR (مبروك) and EN (Congrats) lockups; never stretch, recolor outside palette, or place on low-contrast backgrounds.
- On photography, place the logo on a cream or rose chip for contrast.

### Emotional tone
Joy, warmth, intimacy, surprise. Every asset should feel like the moment a gift is opened — anticipation building to a delighted reveal. Avoid clinical, techy, or stocky visuals; favor real Egyptian faces, real moments, real handwriting-feel where possible.

## 7. Marketing Funnel & The Viral Loop

```
AWARENESS → CONSIDERATION → CONVERSION → RETENTION → REFERRAL
     ▲                                                   │
     └──────────────  every shared link is an ad  ◄──────┘
```

| Stage | Goal | Primary tactics | Key metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Be seen at the moment of an occasion | Reels/TikTok ads, influencer demos, shared links seen in chats | Reach, video views |
| **Consideration** | "I could make this" | Live preview, free tier, occasion landing pages, UGC | Landing→builder rate |
| **Conversion** | Create + (for Premium) pay | Frictionless builder, live preview, InstaPay, urgency on occasions | Activation %, paid conversion % |
| **Retention** | Come back next occasion | Occasion reminders, saved recipients, seasonal nudges | Repeat-create rate |
| **Referral** | Recipient becomes sender | The link itself + soft "Make your own" CTA on every experience | K-factor / share rate |

### The viral loop (the strategic core)
Every experience ends with a tasteful, non-intrusive **"اعمل واحدة زيها — Make your own"** prompt and a branded link. The recipient — already emotionally primed by receiving a delightful moment — is the warmest possible lead. This converts product distribution into a marketing channel:

- **Loop math:** if each sender shares to enough recipients and a fraction become senders, K-factor approaches/exceeds 1.0 and growth compounds without paid spend.
- **Levers we will tune:** the share CTA copy/placement, QR + link friendliness, time-to-first-experience for new arrivals, and occasion timing (Eid sends produce the most downstream loops).
- **Instrument everything:** attribute new senders to the experience that referred them so we can optimize the loop deliberately.

## 10. The Remotion Video Ad Strategy

**Strategic principle:** Our ads should look like the product. Because the product *is* animated, building ads with **Remotion** (programmatic, code-driven video) lets us reuse the exact motion language, fonts, palette, and templates of the app — so an ad is essentially a polished Congrats experience. This delivers three advantages:

1. **Brand-product consistency** — viewers see precisely what they'll make.
2. **Scale & localization** — parametrized compositions generate **bilingual (AR/EN) variants and per-occasion versions programmatically**, with correct RTL — no re-editing by hand.
3. **Cost efficiency** — one composition → many cuts (6s, 15s, 30s) and many occasions, output as vertical 1080×1920 by default for Reels/Stories/TikTok.

### Formats
- **6s bumpers** — single hook + logo; pre-roll/awareness; one emotional beat.
- **15s Reels/Stories/TikTok (vertical 1080×1920)** — the workhorse: hook → personalize → reveal → CTA.
- **30s hero spot** — brand film; the "why," multi-occasion montage, launch + PR use. Also cut to 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9.

Every concept ships in **Arabic (primary) and English**, RTL-correct, palette-locked (rose `#F0436E` lead, gold `#D6A435` for the reveal), on cream surfaces.

### Ad concepts

| # | Occasion | Hook (first 1–2s) | Visual arc | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Graduation** | "مش هتبعتلهم صورة وخلاص؟" / "Just gonna send a photo?" | Boring forwarded image → swipe into animated grad experience with name + cap toss + gold confetti | "اعمل لحظة تخرج — Congrats" |
| 2 | **Eid** | "عيدهم يستاهل أكتر من ستيكر" / "Their Eid deserves more than a sticker" | Generic Eid graphic dissolves → warm animated greeting, lantern motion, family photo, gold flourish | "ابعت فرحة العيد" |
| 3 | **Valentine's / Anniversary** | "قولها بطريقة متتنسيش" / "Say it so they never forget" | Typed text message → blossoms into rose-gradient love experience, couple photo, soft music | "ابعت لحظة حب" |
| 4 | **Proposal** | "اللحظة دي تتبعت إزاي؟" / "How do you send *this* moment?" | Phone reveal → ring-light animation, name, building music to gold climax | "اعمل لحظة الطلب" |
| 5 | **Newborn** | "أهلاً بأحلى خبر" / "Welcome to the sweetest news" | Soft cream scene → baby photo, gentle animation, name reveal, lullaby tone | "ابعت تهنئة المولود" |
| 6 | **Wedding / Multi-occasion (Hero 30s)** | "كل لحظة تستاهل تتبعت صح" / "Every moment deserves to be sent right" | Fast montage across all 8 occasions, names + photos + reveals, ending on logo + "اعمل واحدة" | "ابدأ مجاناً — Start free" |

### Production workflow
- Build a **shared Remotion component library** mirroring the app's templates (intro, personalize-reveal, photo frame, confetti/gold climax, CTA card).
- Parameterize: occasion, language, name, photo, music, format/aspect.
- Render the matrix: 6 concepts × {6s, 15s, 30s where relevant} × {AR, EN} × {9:16, 1:1, 16:9} — programmatically, batched.
- Feed top performers back into the app as featured templates (ad ↔ product flywheel).

- **Goal:** hit 90-day targets; identify the repeatable growth recipe to scale into the next quarter.

## 12. Pricing & Monetization

### Tiers

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 0 EGP | Core occasion experience, personalization, share link + QR (lighter animation, limited/no music) — the funnel entry and loop fuel |
| **Premium** | ~49 EGP+ | Full animation, music, richer templates, premium occasion designs, no/limited branding |

### Strategy
- **Free-to-Premium funnel.** Free removes all friction and powers the viral loop; Premium is an impulse-priced upgrade unlocked at the emotional peak (live preview shows the premium reveal, then prompts to unlock).
- **Occasion bundles / seasonal packs.** Eid family pack, graduation-season pass, Valentine's couples bundle — multi-send value at a slight discount.
- **Gifting credits.** Buy credits to gift Premium creations (and a natural mechanic for ambassador/influencer rewards and contest prizes).
- **Seasonal promos.** Time-boxed discounts and "premium for the price of a coffee" pushes around peak occasions; expat-targeted promos priced for hard-currency ease.
- **Payment.** InstaPay with manual proof upload + admin approval — fits Egyptian norms; streamline the approval queue to keep time-to-delivery low (a conversion-critical operation).

### Monetization principles
- Keep Premium *clearly worth it* (music + full animation are the wow). Never cripple Free so badly the loop dies — Free's job is reach.
- Price psychology: anchor at "a coffee" / "less than a card," lean on emotional value at the moment of decision.
