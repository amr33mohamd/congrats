# Show the guest opening it, then the price

Congrats sells into a big, price-sensitive, WhatsApp-native market, and at 79 EGP it is the cheapest Egyptian web-link wedding invitation found in this research. Egypt registered **936,739 marriage contracts in 2024**. Brides average 25 and grooms 31, and 58% of contracts are rural ([Dostor / CAPMAS](https://www.dostor.org/5325318)). Direct competitors charge **200–800 EGP** for the same "one link with music, map, countdown and RSVP" product ([far7na.me](https://www.far7na.me/), [farahna.net](https://farahna.net/ar), [gettempsite.com](https://www.gettempsite.com/)). One shop lists 100 printed cards at **1,000 EGP** ([Alhadaf Egypt](https://alhadafegypt.net/product/100-%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AA/)). Price is not the hard part. The hard objection is social: elders can see a WhatsApp invitation as an impersonal mass message ([Al Ghad](https://alghad.com/Section-182/%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%A7/%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D9%81%D9%87%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%84%D8%BA%D9%8A-%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%B1%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%B9-1205401)). The guest's own name on the invitation is the answer to that objection. On the ad side, Meta's own guidance is a 9:16 vertical with sound, a hook at the start and key text inside the safe zone. Since the Andromeda retrieval engine, Meta also treats creative diversity as the main way to find audiences ([Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2024/11/07/unlock-the-power-of-reel-ads/), [Atria](https://www.tryatria.com/blog/meta-andromeda)). The recommended ad is a 25-second, result-first product demo built from the existing chat-story and screen-recording assets. The price appears by second 5, the guest's own name shows on screen, and it ends on a Messenger chat bubble. Two other hooks (price shock, and the guest's point of view) share the same body so they can be A/B tested as separate concepts. Several of the numbers below come from single sources or vendor blogs, and the report says so where it matters.

## About 78,000 weddings a month, mostly young, rural and mobile-first

The buyer is young and mobile-first. In 2024 the largest groups were grooms aged 25–30 (41%) and brides aged 20–25 (37%). The most common education level for both is an intermediate certificate, and **57.8% of contracts were rural** ([Dostor / CAPMAS](https://www.dostor.org/5325318)). Ad copy has to read as plain spoken Egyptian Arabic and work outside Cairo's upper middle class. Egypt has **98.2M internet users, 121M mobile connections and a median age of 24.5**. Ad reach is about **51.6M on Facebook, 48.8M on TikTok (+25% year on year), 37.0M on Messenger and 21.7M on Instagram** ([DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-egypt)). Facebook and Messenger are the mass channels, which suits the click-to-Messenger setup. Instagram reaches a smaller, more urban audience. Median mobile download speed is 56 Mbps, so an animated page with music loads fine for most guests ([DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-egypt)).

There is no Egyptian survey of who actually chooses the invitation. A reasonable working guess from the age data is that the bride picks the design, the groom often pays, and mothers shape the guest list. Treat that as a hypothesis to test, not a finding. Rough sizing: about 937k contracts a year is about 78k a month, so a 1% share would be about 9,400 invitations a year. Engagements, katb el-ketab and henna nights add more events per couple, and they are informal enough that WhatsApp is already the natural channel.

The invitation habit is shifting, not gone. Printed cards still sell, from **2 EGP per card** at the budget end ([El-Ekhlas](https://el-ekhlas.site123.me/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%A9/%D9%83%D8%B1%D8%AA-%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%AD-%D9%88%D8%B4%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%A7%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%B2%D8%B1-%D8%A8%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%B1-%D8%AC)) to about 10 EGP per card at a mid-tier online shop. Luxury boxed-card makers are a separate segment on Facebook and Instagram ([Baz IG](https://www.instagram.com/baz_wedding_cards_/)). Regional reporting says COVID made the WhatsApp invitation the norm. One Jordanian printer went from about 1,000 cards per wedding to at most 50 ([Al Ghad](https://alghad.com/Section-182/%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%A7/%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D9%81%D9%87%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%84%D8%BA%D9%8A-%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%B1%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%B9-1205401)). Egyptian families are also trimming costs, and rising hall prices are pushing some celebrations into homes ([Elwatan News](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8251483)). The likely pattern, inferred because no usage survey exists, is a few printed cards for elders and VIPs and WhatsApp for everyone else. Congrats should position itself as the better WhatsApp layer, not as a full replacement for printed cards.

## Eid drives weddings, and Ramadan is the dead zone where greeting cards take over

Egyptian wedding demand jumps right after Eid al-Fitr, and Ramadan has "almost no work" for wedding vendors ([Elwatan News](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8251483)). Many provincial grooms work abroad and come home on Eid leave, which concentrates weddings around the holidays ([Noon Post](https://www.noonpost.com/28910/)). Gulf data shows summer as another peak ([Al Etihad](https://www.aletihad.ae/news/الإمارات/4503949/9-قاعات-أفراح-لاستقبال-حجوزات-الصيف-في-العين)). Monthly Egyptian marriage data was not found, so it is unknown whether October–December is a secondary peak.

The practical consequence is that the two product lines cover each other's off-seasons. Invitations are sent about 2–4 weeks before the event, so invitation ads should start about a month before each Eid peak. Greeting cards (free–49 EGP) peak exactly when weddings stop: Ramadan, both Eids, Mother's Day on 21 March, and results and graduation season in July–August. Meta reports that **65% of people observing Ramadan watch more video during Ramadan and Eid** ([Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/ramadan-business-insights-a-guide-to-meaningful-customer-engagement/)). Using approximate Hijri dates that are not from a source here, Ramadan 2027 runs about 8 February to 9 March and Eid al-Fitr falls around 10 March. That points to this sequence: invitation ads now through January, a switch to greeting-card creative for Ramadan and Eid, and invitation ads again from late February aimed at post-Eid weddings. A "حضّر دعوتك قبل العيد" (get your invitation ready before Eid) urgency message fits naturally before each Eid.

From their landing pages, though, nobody in Egypt appears to own the emotional demo of a guest opening the invitation on a phone. Congrats already has that asset in its chat-story and hand-opening animation.

## Personalisation beats the "mass message" objection, and the free preview beats the scam fear

Payments have mostly stopped being the problem. Egypt has **46.3M active mobile wallets (+29% year on year), and Vodafone Cash handles 78% of wallet transactions** ([NTRA Q2 2025](https://www.tra.gov.eg/en/ntra-issues-q2-2025-report-on-the-usage-indicators-of-mobile-wallets-operated-by-telecom-companies/)). InstaPay reached about **12M users by end-2024** ([Banker.news](https://www.banker.news/89657)) and has charged a small transfer fee since April 2025 ([Egyptian Streets](https://egyptianstreets.com/2025/04/03/instapay-now-charging-transfer-fees)). Distrust of online payment and a cash-on-delivery habit are fading but still present ([Egyptian Streets](https://egyptianstreets.com/2026/06/24/the-rise-of-online-shopping-how-e-commerce-became-one-of-egypts-fastest-growing-industries/)). For a 79 EGP digital product, the scam fear is better answered by "see your design free before you pay" than by any payment badge. Congrats only takes InstaPay, while Vodafone Cash dominates wallet usage, so adding Vodafone Cash is an easy friction cut.

The deeper objection is etiquette. A Jordanian sociologist says printed cards carried "appreciation and esteem" through personalised names. An elderly woman in the same report refused a WhatsApp invitation as a generic mass message ([Al Ghad](https://alghad.com/Section-182/%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%A7/%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D9%81%D9%87%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%84%D8%BA%D9%8A-%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%B1%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%B9-1205401)). A Saudi forum openly asks whether a WhatsApp photo of a card is enough to justify attending ([Hawamer](https://hawamer.com/vb/hawamer2795370-4)). This evidence is regional, not Egyptian, but it identifies the exact fix: the objection is to impersonality, not to the digital format. Congrats' guest name and the two family names are therefore the main message, not a small feature. The messaging angles below are inferred from this evidence and have not been tested, so A/B test them. Saving money: "أرخص من ١٠ كروت مطبوعة" (cheaper than 10 printed cards), defensible against the 1,000 EGP per 100 card listing, though that comes from one shop. Elegance: "مش مجرد صورة على الواتساب" (not just a picture on WhatsApp). Respect: "اسم كل ضيف على دعوته" (each guest's name on their own invitation). Practical: "اللوكيشن بضغطة، محدش هيتوه" (the location in one tap, nobody gets lost). Relatives abroad: "اللي برة مصر يوصله في ثانية" (relatives outside Egypt get it in a second). Speed against freelancers: "جاهزة في دقايق مش أيام" (ready in minutes, not days). Avoid naming competitors in ads. If a comparison is wanted, "أقل من نص سعر مواقع الدعوات التانية" (less than half the price of other invitation sites) is accurate against all four Egyptian sites found.

## Meta rewards sound-on vertical demos, distinct concepts and an obvious chat CTA

Meta's Reels "creative essentials" are full-screen 9:16, built for sound, a hook at the start, and key message and CTA inside the safe zone. Campaigns that followed them got **2X higher delivery to Reels**, and **over 75% of Instagram Reels views are sound-on** ([Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2024/11/07/unlock-the-power-of-reel-ads/)). Meta gives no ideal length. Vendor guidance converges on **15–30 seconds, with 21–24 seconds as a sweet spot**, and says completion drops after 30 seconds ([adstellar.ai](https://www.adstellar.ai/blog/video-ad-length-best-practices)). The benchmark to watch is hook rate (3-second plays divided by impressions). Below 25% the opening needs work, and 30–35% or more is strong ([Motion](https://motionapp.com/blog/key-creative-performance-metrics), [Exactius](https://exacti.us/growth-library/thumb-stop-rate)). TikTok's older but still useful finding is that **63% of top-CTR ads show the product or key message in the first 3 seconds** ([TikTok for Business](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance)). Captions should be burned in even with sound-on, because about a quarter of viewers are muted and Arabic auto-captions of Egyptian dialect are unreliable (inferred). Meta itself recommends timed text ([Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/ramadan-business-insights-a-guide-to-meaningful-customer-engagement/)).

Safe-zone guidance conflicts. The conservative standard keeps the **top 14%, bottom 35% and sides 6%** clear, about 270px top, 672px bottom and 65px each side on 1080x1920 ([House of Marketers](https://houseofmarketers.com/guide-to-safe-zones-tiktok-facebook-instagram-stories-reels/)). A looser pixel version uses 108px top, 320px bottom, 60px left and 120px right ([adnova.ai](https://adnova.ai/blogs/meta-ad-safe-zones-guide)). Meta reportedly unified the Stories and Reels zones in March 2026, but that comes from a vendor ([Billo](https://billo.app/?p=50654)). Design to the strictest version: all text and the price between **y≈300 and y≈1200px**, within **x≈80–960px**. That respects the 120px right margin, which matters because right-aligned Arabic starts right where Reels places its like, comment and share icons.

On format, 2025 trend reports favour lo-fi, native-looking, product-in-use video ([Motion via BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250429844196/en)). Polished branded creative sometimes wins, though: in one case CPA was $18 against $29 for UGC ([Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/how-to-analyze-meta-ad-performance-with-motion-sans-case-study/)). A real screen recording shown in a hand-held phone frame gets both the native feel and a clear look at the product. For click-to-message, Meta's guidance is to **make it clear that tapping starts a conversation** and to reply fast ([WhatsApp Business](https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/resource-library/how-to-create-successful-ads-that-click-to-whatsapp/)). Messenger ads support a custom greeting, **icebreakers (title up to 80 characters, reply up to 300)** and a prefilled message ([Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/ads)). A Meta MENA case got **2X leads at 56% lower cost per lead** from chat ads ([WhatsApp Business](https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/success-stories/arabian-automobiles-company/)). No Egyptian case study was found.

Andromeda, Meta's ad retrieval engine since December 2024, changes how variants should be built. Meta said in April 2025 that **creative diversification is now "the best lever to find the most relevant audiences"** ([Atria](https://www.tryatria.com/blog/meta-andromeda)). Vendors report that near-duplicate ads (swapping colour, one word or the music) get grouped and treated as one ad, while different hooks, personas and angles count as separate concepts. They recommend 5–10 distinct concepts in one broad ad set ([Atria](https://www.tryatria.com/blog/meta-andromeda)). Neither the grouping mechanics nor the numbers appear in a primary Meta source. For a small budget, run three very different hook openings on a shared body in one broad ad set, and optimise for conversations. For low-ticket impulse buys, peer-reviewed 2025 research confirms that both the quantity and quality of social proof drive impulse buying from short video ([Huang et al., JRCS 2025](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/joreco/v84y2025ics0969698924004958.html)). Congrats has no real testimonials yet, so the brief uses the honest substitute: the product demonstrating itself, labelled as a demo. No source tests showing the price early, but at 79 EGP the price is a reason to buy, so show it by second 5.

## Creative brief: "الدعوة اللي بتتفتح" (the invitation that opens), 25 seconds, 1080x1920

**Objective and setup.** Click-to-Messenger, optimising for conversations, in one broad ad set with this ad and the two alternative hooks. Audience: Egypt, 20–35, broad. Do not stack interests, because Andromeda finds the audience from the creative. Greeting: "أهلاً بيك في Congrats 💍 تحب تعمل دعوة فرح ولا تهنئة؟" (Welcome to Congrats 💍 Would you like a wedding invitation or a greeting card?). Prefilled message: "عايز أعمل دعوة فرح" (I want to make a wedding invitation). Icebreakers: "بكام الدعوة؟" (how much is the invitation?), "عايز أشوف التصميمات" (I want to see the designs), "تعملوهالي إنتو؟" (can you make it for me?), "الدفع إزاي؟" (how do I pay?). The second icebreaker replies with /ar/t/<slug> preview links. The third routes to the "we make it for you" service.

**Assets.** Existing WhatsApp-style chat animation with a hand opening the link, real screen recordings of Golden Era, Ivory Arch, Qasr Gold, Baroque Noir, Blue Porcelain and Sage Garden, and a Messenger-style bubble for the end card. Every name on screen is clearly sample data (for example "أحمد & سارة", families "عائلة الشريف وعائلة منصور"), and a small persistent label "عرض توضيحي" (demo) runs in the top band. Use no reviews, counts or "customers love it" claims.

**Sound.** The Golden Era tarab-style track gives an instant Egyptian wedding signal for the first 8 seconds. The music must be licensed or original, because copyrighted tarab recordings risk Meta muting the ad. After that, cut to an upbeat track at about 120+ BPM, matching TikTok's VTR finding. Use a warm female Egyptian-colloquial voiceover in a casual voice-note tone, not MSA, plus soft UI sound effects: a WhatsApp ping, a tap, a whoosh on the open.

**Safe-zone layout.** Headline text sits in the **upper band, y 300–520px**. The phone or screen recording fills the **centre, about y 420–1200px**. Price and CTA chips sit at **y 1000–1200px**. Nothing important goes below **y 1248px** or above **y 270px**, and text stays within **x 80–960px**. Arabic is right-aligned with an inset from the right edge. Use a bold, rounded Arabic display font, white on a soft dark scrim, at no more than about 7 words per line.

The structure follows the evidence. The product appears in the first frame (result-first). The emotional answer to the "mass message" objection comes by second 3. The price comes by second 5. The map and RSVP beats show what an MP4 or a JPEG of a printed card cannot do. The end card's chat bubble does what Meta asks for: it makes plain that tapping opens a conversation. It also offers the done-for-you service to buyers who won't fill in a form. If the cut needs to be 20 seconds, merge the countdown beat into the price beat and trim the montage to 3 designs.

### Alternative hook B: price shock (0–3s, swaps in for 0–2.5s)

Visual: a stack of printed cards and a receipt reading "١٠٠ كارت = ١٠٠٠ جنيه" (100 cards = 1,000 EGP). A phone slides in front of it and the Golden Era invitation opens. Text: **لسه هتطبع ١٠٠ كارت بألف جنيه؟** (Still printing 100 cards for 1,000 EGP?) VO: "ليه تدفع ألف جنيه كروت؟ دعوتك على الموبايل بـ٧٩ بس" (why pay 1,000 EGP for cards? your invitation on the phone is just 79). This leads with the angle the regional coverage treats as the main argument for digital, and suits the cost-conscious rural and popular-wedding segment. The 1,000 EGP figure comes from one shop's listing, so word it as "لحد ألف جنيه" (up to 1,000 EGP) if challenged.

### Alternative hook C: the guest's point of view (0–3s)

Visual: an over-the-shoulder phone shot (or the hand animation) of an older relative's WhatsApp. The invitation opens on "دعوة خاصة لـ الحاجة سعاد" (A special invitation for Hajja Soad), a hand goes to the heart, and the RSVP is tapped. Text: **لما خالتك تفتح دعوة فرحك وتلاقي اسمها عليها 🥹** (When your aunt opens your wedding invitation and finds her name on it 🥹). VO: "الدعوة اللي بتحسس كل واحد إنه متعزوم بجد" (the invitation that makes everyone feel truly invited). This hook goes straight at the "impersonal mass message" objection with a different persona and emotion, so Andromeda should treat it as a distinct concept rather than a variant. If a real person is filmed, use a clearly staged scene and keep the "عرض توضيحي" (demo) label, because nothing should suggest a real customer reaction.

Judge the three hooks first on hook rate (target above 25–30%), then on cost per conversation and the share of conversations that reach payment. If one wins clearly, make new concepts in that direction rather than recolouring it.

## Conclusion

The research changes where the effort should go. Congrats has already solved price, sitting at a third to a tenth of every Egyptian link competitor and a fraction of printed cards. The open question is whether a guest, especially an older one, feels honoured or spammed. So the creative should lead with the guest's experience (their name, the map, the one-tap RSVP) and use 79 EGP as the closer, not the headline. Hook C is probably the most strategically valuable test even if B wins on cheap clicks. The calendar matters just as much. With Ramadan 2027 starting in early February, the business needs greeting-card creative ready before then and a post-Eid invitation push from late February. That makes the two product lines one year-round ad program rather than two separate products.

The biggest unknowns are Egyptian-specific. No one has measured who decides on the invitation, monthly wedding seasonality, or how guests treat links compared with videos. The first few hundred Messenger conversations will answer those questions more cheaply than any further desk research, so tag each conversation by hook, event type and who is writing (bride, groom or parent). Once real buyers agree, their screenshots and RSVPs become the honest social proof this brief deliberately leaves out.
