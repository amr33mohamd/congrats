# WhatsApp & Facebook Groups — Congrats

This is how things actually spread in Egypt/MENA. Keep it short, forwardable, and
value-first. **Include a real demo link** — people forward the *thing*, not the
pitch. Attach the hero GIF where the channel allows it.

**Rules of the road:**
- Only post in groups where you're already a genuine member. Cold-dropping links
  in groups you've never engaged with gets you removed and hurts the brand.
- Lead with something useful/nice, not "please try my app."
- Don't blast the same message to 20 chats at once — WhatsApp flags it as spam and
  can restrict your number. Send in small, personal batches.
- Wedding invitations are the flagship: lead with `[demo invitation link]` whenever
  the person is engaged, planning, or a wedding vendor. Use the card messages
  (further down) for everyone else.
