# Congrats — Marketing Launch Kit

Ready-to-post drafts for launching **Congrats** — a web app to build a step-by-step
**animated greeting** (birthday, wedding, anniversary, valentine, proposal, Eid,
graduation, newborn) with the recipient's name, your photos, and your words, then
share it as a single link. Bilingual (English + Arabic/RTL), mobile-first, no install.
Free templates + premium templates paid via **InstaPay** (Egypt, EGP).

> These are **drafts for you to review and post yourself.** Fill in every

## Launch-day checklist (12 steps, in order)

Do steps 1-4 **in the week before** launch. Steps 5-12 are the launch day itself.

**Pre-launch prep (the week before)**

1. **Stand up analytics.** Add a lightweight, privacy-friendly analytics tool
   (Plausible, Umami, or PostHog) so you can see where launch-day traffic comes
   from. Tag your links with UTM parameters (e.g.
   `?utm_source=producthunt&utm_medium=launch`) so you know what actually worked.
2. **Set up a real support email** (`[support email]`) and make sure it forwards
   to a phone you check. On launch day, speed of reply = trust. Add it to the
   site footer and the PH page.
3. **Make 2-3 "hero" demo cards** — one birthday, one wedding, one Eid — that
   genuinely look great. These become your screenshots, GIFs, and the
   `[demo experience link]` you paste everywhere. Test them on a cheap Android
   phone, not just your own device.
4. **Warm up an audience.** 5-7 days out, start posting "building in public"
   teasers on X/Instagram (a 3-second clip of confetti firing, a name typing in).
   DM 15-20 friends who'll genuinely reply on launch morning and ask them —
   personally, not in a group blast — to check it out and comment/upvote when you
   go live. Line up 1-2 people to be your first "hunter"/first comment on PH.

**Launch day (run top to bottom)**

5. **12:01am PT — go live on Product Hunt.** PH days start at midnight Pacific.
   Post `product-hunt.md`, then immediately add your maker's first comment. Pin it.
6. **Post the Product Hunt link to X** (the launch announcement tweet from
   `x-twitter.md`) and quote-tweet it with the thread. Pin the announcement to
   your profile.
7. **Send the personal DMs** you lined up in step 4 — "we're live, here's the
   link." Personal asks convert; mass tags get ignored.
8. **Post the LinkedIn founder story** (`linkedin.md`). Engage with every comment
   in the first two hours — the algorithm rewards early replies.
9. **Send the launch email** (`email-announcement.md`) to friends/family/early
   list. Pick the Arabic or English version per contact.
10. **Send the WhatsApp / Facebook-group messages** (`whatsapp-and-groups.md`) —
    this is how things actually spread in Egypt/MENA. Post value-first in groups
    where you're already a member; don't cold-drop links in groups you've never
    engaged with (you'll get banned).
11. **Post the Reddit threads** (`reddit.md`) to r/SideProject and one other fit.
    Space them out; reply to every comment like a human, not a brand.
12. **Post the short-form videos** (`instagram-tiktok.md`) to Reels + TikTok, and
    reshare the best clip to your Story with a "swipe up / link in bio." Then
    watch analytics, reply to everyone, and thank every single person who shared.

**After launch:** for the next 5 days, post one standalone tweet/day (see
`x-twitter.md`), keep replying to comments, and DM anyone who made a card asking
what they'd improve. Momentum on day 2-5 matters more than the day-1 spike.
