# Instagram Reels / TikTok — Congrats

Format: vertical 9:16, 15-30s, fast cuts, screen recording of the app + a real
reaction. Text-on-screen carries the story (most people watch muted). Hook in the
first 1.5 seconds or they scroll. Use a trending, upbeat/emotional audio.

Post the same video to both Reels and TikTok; put the link in bio + "link in bio"
sticker.

---

## Video 1 — "Make a card in 60 seconds" (the core demo)

**Length:** ~25s · **Vibe:** satisfying, fast, a little wholesome.

| Time | On screen | Text overlay | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-2s | Boring WhatsApp chat, someone types "Happy bday 🎉" | "stop sending this." | The hook is the contrast. |
| 2-5s | Cut to Congrats: tap "Birthday" | "make this instead 👇" | |
| 5-10s | Type a name, drop in 2 photos | "her name. your photos." | Speed up 2x. |
| 10-14s | Type a short message | "your words." | |
| 14-16s | Tap "create" → "your link is ready" | "one link." | |
| 16-22s | The finished card plays: name bounces, photos fade, confetti | *(let it breathe)* | This is the payoff — full volume moment. |
| 22-25s | Paste link into WhatsApp, hit send | "made in 60 seconds. free to try." | End card: logo + [site URL] handle |

## Video 2 — "Watch them react" (emotional, POV)

**Length:** ~20s · **Vibe:** heartfelt.

- 0-2s: POV of a phone buzzing, a WhatsApp notification. Overlay: **"my mom on her birthday →"**
- 2-4s: Finger taps the link.
- 4-14s: Screen-record the card playing — her name in Arabic, old family photos fading in, "كل سنة وإنت طيب," confetti. Let the animation carry it.
- 14-18s: Cut to a real reaction shot (someone smiling/tearing up at their phone). Overlay: **"she called me crying 🥹"**
- 18-20s: End card. Overlay: **"make one for someone → link in bio."**

*(If you don't have a reaction clip, use a genuine screenshot of a "😭❤️" reply
in the chat instead.)*

## Video 3 — "Every occasion" (range + speed)

**Length:** ~15s · **Vibe:** punchy montage.

- 0-2s: Fast flick through the template gallery. Overlay: **"whatever you're celebrating…"**
- 2-12s: 1-second beats, each a different finished card firing its confetti moment — birthday, wedding, Eid ("عيد مبارك"), graduation, newborn ("مبروك المولود"), anniversary. Cut on the beat of the audio.
- 12-15s: End card. Overlay: **"animated cards, sent as a link. free templates → [site URL]"**
