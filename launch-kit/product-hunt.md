## Best day / time to launch

- **Day:** Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday. Avoid Monday (crowded) and the
  weekend (low traffic). Never launch on a US holiday.
- **Time:** **12:01am Pacific Time (PT)** — PH resets its day at midnight PT, so
  posting right at the start gives you the full 24 hours to accumulate upvotes.
  For Egypt (EET) that's around **9-10am** depending on daylight saving — good,
  because it lines up with your MENA audience waking up.
- Be online and responsive for the first 3-4 hours; the early curve sets the day.
