# SEO Keywords — Congrats

Target search phrases people would actually type when looking for what Congrats
does. Grouped by intent. Use these in page titles, headings, template page copy,
meta descriptions, and blog posts. Prioritize the **high-intent** ones (someone
ready to make/send a card) for landing and template pages.

The live gallery (`/ar/templates`, `/en/templates`) is the best landing page for
all of these today — every template plays in the grid. Invitation keywords come
first because invitations are the paid flagship.

## Notes on how to use these

- Give each occasion its own landing page (e.g. `/ar/templates?category=invitation`
  or dedicated `/ar/invitations`, `/ar/eid`) and target the matching high-intent
  phrase in the page title and H1. (Dedicated occasion pages and a blog don't exist
  yet — see `docs/MARKETING_PLAN.md` §13.)
- Because the site is Arabic-default, make sure Arabic keyword pages have proper
  `lang`/`dir` and hreflang tags so search engines serve the right language.
- Anchor Arabic terms: **«دعوة فرح إلكترونية»** for invitations and **«تهنئة متحركة»**
  for cards — work them into titles, alt text and template names.
- Search Console (see `docs/LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md` §K) will show which of these you
  actually rank for after ~6 weeks; re-prioritise then.

## Blog-post title ideas (content marketing)

1. **"Stop sending 'Happy Birthday 🎉': 7 ways to make a birthday message they'll
   actually keep"** — targets gifting/idea searchers, ends with your tool as the
   easiest option. (Arabic version: **"بلاش 'كل سنة وإنت طيب' ناشفة: 7 طرق تخلي
   تهنئتك تفضل معاهم"**.)

2. **"How to send an animated greeting card as a link (no app, works on any
   phone)"** — a genuine how-to that ranks for the high-intent "send as a link"
   queries and naturally demos Congrats.

3. **"Wedding season survival guide: what to send instead of the 40th 'mabrook'"**
   — seasonal, shareable, targets wedding-gift and congratulations searches in
   both EN and AR. (Arabic: **"دليل موسم الأفراح: تبعت إيه غير الـ'مبروك' رقم 40"**.)

4. **«صيغة دعوة فرح: ١٥ صيغة جاهزة بالعامية والفصحى»** — the informational query
   with the most volume in the invitation space; every صيغة ends with "put it in a
   Congrats invitation".

5. **«دعوة فرح إلكترونية ولا مطبوعة؟ مقارنة التكلفة والوقت»** — honest comparison;
   recommends printing a few for elders and sending the link to everyone else.

6. **«إزاي تبعت دعوة الفرح على الواتساب من غير ما تبان سبام»** — practical tips
   (small batches, personal line first, family group before everyone).
