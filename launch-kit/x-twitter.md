## Launch thread (6 tweets)

**1 — hook**
> We just launched Congrats.
>
> It turns a name and a few photos into an animated greeting you share as one
> link. Here's why I built it 🧵

**2 — problem**
> Real moments — birthdays, weddings, a friend's baby, someone graduating — keep
> getting flattened into "🎉🎉🎉" in a group chat.
>
> Making something more personal means fighting a video editor for an hour. Most
> people give up and send the emoji.

**3a — the invitation**
> The flagship: wedding invitations.
>
> Guest taps a seal → music starts → one scroll through the families, the date
> with a countdown, the couple's photos, the venue (one tap → Google Maps), RSVP
> straight to the couple's WhatsApp, and gift details.
>
> [attach invitation GIF / demo invitation link]

**3 — solution (cards)**
> Congrats does the hard part.
>
> Pick an occasion → type their name → drop in a few photos + your message. It
> builds a step-by-step animated card: text arrives, photos fade in, confetti at
> the end. You get one link.

**4 — demo**
> This is a real one, start to finish 👇
>
> Made in under a minute. Open it on any phone — no app, no sign-up for whoever
> receives it. Just tap and watch.
>
> [attach demo GIF or paste demo experience link]
