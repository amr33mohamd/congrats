# Competitive Landscape: Digital / Video Wedding Invitations & E-Cards for Arabic-Speaking Customers (Egypt first), 2025–2026

Researched 2026-10-07. Prices are as shown on the pages at fetch time. USD→EGP conversions use an approximate 1 USD ≈ 48–50 EGP. That rate is an assumption I did not check against a source in this research, so verify it before you use these figures in ad copy.

## 1. Who sells e-invitations in Egypt (and the Arab world)?

### Takeaway
At least four Egyptian self-serve web-link invitation sites sell in EGP: far7na.me, farahna.net, da3wa.online and temp.site. far7na.me is the closest direct competitor to Congrats, because it uses Egyptian dialect, sells a single link with RSVP, map, countdown and music, and has InstaPay/wallet payment visible. The rest of the market is freelance sellers on Khamsat/Mostaql (video MP4, image or PDF, and custom web pages), Instagram/WhatsApp designers (many are Saudi), free template tools (Canva, Saudi "Ethraa"/my-card.one), Gulf guest-management apps with QR entry (Mazoom, Hayyacom, da3watfarah.com) and Western platforms (Paperless Post, Greenvelope, Evite) that hardly target Egypt.

### Cited Findings
**Egyptian self-serve web-link platforms (direct competitors)**
- **far7na.me ("فرحنا")**: describes itself as the first Egyptian site for online wedding invitations ("اعمل دعوة فرح أونلاين"). It sells one package at **200 EGP**, shown as an intro offer against a "regular" **500 EGP**. Payment is one-time with no subscription. Features: RSVP with attendance and guest counts, Google Maps, countdown in days and hours, romantic auto-playing music, editable names/date/venue/photos, a guest wall for congratulation messages, a unique link (far7na.me/[couple-names]), editing after purchase, and the link stays live until 3 days after the wedding. Printed cards are quoted on WhatsApp. Copy is in Egyptian colloquial Arabic. InstaPay and a wallet appear in the site's payment settings — [far7na.me](https://www.far7na.me/)
- **farahna.net ("فرحنا — دعوات رقمية للمناسبات")**, Egypt, priced in EGP. Designing and previewing is free, and you pay once when you publish. Packages: Free (design and preview only), **Bliss 399 EGP** (music, basic features) and **Forever 599 EGP** (music, RSVP, guest messages, photo gallery, QR code). No account is needed. The site claims an invitation is ready in under 3 minutes. Its homepage does not mention a countdown or a map — [farahna.net/ar](https://farahna.net/ar)
- **da3wa.online ("دعوة أونلاين")** calls itself the "#1 Choice for Weddings in Egypt" and also sells to the UAE, KSA, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman. "E-Invite Starter" starts at **675 EGP**. "WhatsApp Auto" sending starts at **22 EGP per guest**. Features: personalised WhatsApp sending by guest name, RSVP via in-chat buttons or on the page, a personal QR entry code (Diamond tier), automated reminders and thank-you messages, Arabic and English on one page, a live dashboard, an envelope-opening animation, countdown and map. Payment is Apple Pay, Google Pay or Visa/Mastercard; InstaPay is not shown. It claims 4,000+ events and a 4.9/5 rating — [da3wa.online](https://da3wa.online/en)
- **temp.site (gettempsite.com)** is an Egyptian web agency. Wedding invitation sites cost **400–800 EGP**, with add-ons at **200 EGP each**. Features: photo gallery, countdown, venue map, comments. The link is live from 1 week before to 1 week after the event. Payment is InstaPay, Vodafone Cash or e& wallet. Templates take 4–7 days to deliver — [gettempsite.com](https://www.gettempsite.com/)

**Arab-world self-serve platforms**
- **da3watfarah.com ("دعوة فرح")** targets KSA, the UAE and the wider Arab world. Plans: Free, **Premium $150** one-time lifetime (~7,200–7,500 EGP) and **VIP $500**. Features: RSVP, Google Maps, countdown, uploading your own music, unlimited gallery, AI text generation, QR codes and a custom domain (VIP). It claims 10,000+ invitations, 24,000+ guests and 98% satisfaction — [da3watfarah.com](https://da3watfarah.com/)
- **Mazoom (Saudi, iOS app)** prices per guest list. In-app purchases: 10 guests $19.99, 50 guests $49.99, 100 guests $69.99, 200 guests $179.99, 500 guests $349.99, Premium subscription $11.99, Unlimited $49.99 — [App Store via search result](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1604235156). The App Store page itself returned HTTP 429, so these figures come from the search snippet.
- Mostaql project briefs name **Mazoom, Hayyacom and "حياك"** as the reference products for Saudi QR-entry invitation systems: a unique per-guest QR scanned once at the door, sending by WhatsApp or email, and RSVP chatbots — [mostaql.com/project/1007858](https://mostaql.com/project/1007858)

**Freelance marketplaces (Khamsat / Mostaql / Nafezly), prices in USD**

**Instagram / social sellers**

**Free / DIY tools**
- **Canva** has a free Arabic page for making wedding invitation videos online ("تصميم دعوة زفاف فيديو أونلاين مجاناً") — [canva.com](https://www.canva.com/create/wedding-invitation-videos/)
- **my-card.one ("إثراء")**, Saudi: **free**, no sign-up, no watermark. Offers 30 editable templates (Najdi, Hijazi and Moroccan motifs) that export as image (for WhatsApp) or PDF (for print), all made in the browser — [my-card.one](https://my-card.one/wedding-invit/)

**International platforms**
- **Paperless Post** uses coins. Free designs go to up to 50 recipients. Premium cards are about 2 coins per guest, with 300 coins costing $50 (about $0.60–$1.15 per guest) — [Joy / rsvpify / 1invites roundups](https://rsvpify.com/top-paperless-post-alternatives/)

### Inferences
- **Congrats at 79 EGP undercuts every Egyptian web-link competitor I found.** far7na.me's promo is 200 EGP (2.5x), farahna.net starts at 399 EGP (5x) for a paid link, temp.site charges 400–800 EGP and da3wa.online starts at 675 EGP. The cheapest Khamsat web card ($5) costs about 3x as much and takes a day of back-and-forth with a freelancer.
- far7na.me is the most direct overlap: the same dialect, the same "one link" pitch and the same InstaPay/wallet rails. Its "500 → 200 EGP" anchor tells Congrats what Egyptian buyers see as normal. Congrats can say "less than half the price of other invitation sites".
- At the premium end (da3wa.online, Mazoom, da3watfarah), the value is guest management: per-guest WhatsApp sending, QR door entry and reminders. Congrats should not compete there at 79 EGP. It should frame itself as the instant, beautiful, affordable option.
- Video MP4 invitations from freelancers cost about 250–3,700 EGP ($5–$75) and take 2–6 days to deliver, so instant delivery is an obvious thing for Congrats to stress.

### Gaps
- I could not reach the Hayyacom website or its pricing (the search returned unrelated results).
- I found no public price lists from Egyptian Instagram/TikTok video-invitation designers, who usually say "السعر في الخاص" (price by DM). Their prices could not be verified.
- The App Store listings for Mazoom, Zola and Joy were rate-limited or not fetched. Zola and Joy pricing and features were not covered.
- I did not find an app literally named "Da3wa". The closest matches are da3wa.online (Egypt and Gulf) and da3watfarah.com.

## 2. Prices, delivery time, formats, customization, RSVP

### Takeaway
Egyptian web-link invitations cost 200–800 EGP, plus per-guest WhatsApp sending at da3wa.online. Freelance MP4 videos cost $5–$75 with 1–6 day turnaround. Free static image/PDF tools exist, as do Gulf apps priced per guest ($20 for 10 guests up to $350 for 500). RSVP, map, countdown and music are now standard on web links, and none of them exist in MP4/image formats.

## 3. How competitors advertise (claims and hooks)

### Takeaway
Hooks fall into a few groups: everything in one link, fast/in minutes, cheaper than paper, a story or memory, one click on WhatsApp, and Gulf "first/#1" authority claims with social-proof stats. I could not access Meta Ad Library or TikTok captions, so the hooks below are taken from landing pages and listings.

### Cited Findings
- far7na.me: "دعوتك لفرحك، في لينك واحد" (your wedding invitation in one link). It positions itself as the "smartest and most economical option" against paper cards and static WhatsApp images, and claims to be "أول موقع مصري" (the first Egyptian site) — [far7na.me](https://www.far7na.me/)
- farahna.net: "كل تفاصيل فرحكم… في دعوة واحدة" (all your wedding details in one invitation), "مش بس رابط… ذكرى" (not just a link, a memory), plus free design and preview with payment once on publish — [farahna.net/ar](https://farahna.net/ar)
- da3watfarah.com: "المنصة الأولى لدعوات الزفاف في العالم العربي" (the first wedding invitation platform in the Arab world), "اصنع دعوة زفاف تحكي قصتك" (make an invitation that tells your story), "في دقائق معدودة" (in just a few minutes), and the stats 10,000+ invitations and 98% satisfaction — [da3watfarah.com](https://da3watfarah.com/)
- da3wa.online: "#1 Choice for Weddings in Egypt", 4,000+ events, 4.9/5, and an emphasis on time saved and orderly QR door entry — [da3wa.online](https://da3wa.online/en)
- A Khamsat video seller: "ترسله بضغطة واحدة على كل وسائل التواصل" (send it with one tap on every social app), pitched as the modern alternative to traditional invitations — [Khamsat 4215903](https://khamsat.com/video-design/invitations-greetings/4215903)
- Video-invitation marketing commonly says it is "much cheaper than paper cards" and sendable in one click via WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram (search summary of listings) — [Khamsat 4215903](https://khamsat.com/video-design/invitations-greetings/4215903)
- Free tools lead with "مجانًا وبلا تسجيل" (free, no sign-up) and "بلا علامة مائية" (no watermark) — [my-card.one](https://my-card.one/wedding-invit/)
- Instagram sellers such as @riy2u rely on highlight reels (samples, testimonials, modification policy) and take orders on WhatsApp DM — [instagram.com/riy2u](https://www.instagram.com/riy2u/)

### Inferences
- "One link" is already claimed by far7na.me and farahna.net, so Congrats needs a sharper angle. Options: price ("بـ79 جنيه بس"), speed ("جاهزة في دقيقة، من غير ما تستنى ديزاينر"), and the guest experience (the guest sees their own name, opens the map and replies on WhatsApp).
- Nobody in Egypt seems to own the "the guest opens it like a real envelope" emotional demo in short video form. Congrats' Remotion chat-story and hand-opening videos fit that gap. This is inferred from landing pages only, without ad-library access.

### Gaps
- I had no access to Meta Ad Library, TikTok captions or view counts. Actual ad creatives, spend and run length are unknown.

## 4. Web-link vs video-MP4 invitation: gaps and differentiators

### Takeaway
A web link can do things an MP4 cannot. It can open a live map, run a countdown, collect RSVPs (to WhatsApp or a dashboard), play music, show the guest's own name, be edited after sending and play instantly without a download. Video still wins on cinematic emotion, and it autoplays in WhatsApp, Status and Reels feeds.

### Cited Findings
- Web-link competitors bundle RSVP, Google Maps, countdown, music, gallery and guest messages, plus editing after purchase on far7na.me — [far7na.me](https://www.far7na.me/), [farahna.net](https://farahna.net/ar), [da3watfarah.com](https://da3watfarah.com/)
- Personalised sending by guest name, with RSVP buttons inside WhatsApp, is a paid premium feature (22 EGP per guest) at da3wa.online — [da3wa.online](https://da3wa.online/en)
- Freelance MP4 videos are fixed once rendered. Changes go through limited revision rounds (2–3), a new date or venue means re-rendering, and delivery takes 2–6 days — [Khamsat 4215903](https://khamsat.com/video-design/invitations-greetings/4215903), [Khamsat 4447955](https://khamsat.com/designing/wedding-invitation/4447955)
- Some video sellers add a QR for the venue location as a workaround for the missing map link — [Khamsat 3848945](https://khamsat.com/designing/wedding-invitation/3848945)
- A video seller upsells "convert to an HTML website" for +$50, which shows that sellers themselves treat a website as the higher tier — [Khamsat 4215903](https://khamsat.com/video-design/invitations-greetings/4215903)

### Inferences
- Messages Congrats can own against video:
  1. "اسم كل ضيف على دعوته" (each guest's name on their invitation), which only da3wa.online offers and at a much higher price.
  2. "الضيف يرد عليك على الواتساب بضغطة" (the guest replies to you on WhatsApp in one tap).
  3. "اللوكيشن يفتح على الخريطة على طول" (the location opens straight in the map, no QR hunting).
  4. "غيّرت الميعاد؟ عدّل من غير ما تبعت تاني" (changed the time? edit it without resending).
  5. "جاهزة في دقايق مش أيام" (ready in minutes, not days).
  6. "79 جنيه بدل 1000 جنيه لفيديو" (79 EGP instead of 1,000 for a video).
- Hybrid tactic: a short MP4 or animated preview is the WhatsApp/Status hook that autoplays, and the link is the payload. That addresses the one real advantage video has.
- Risks: guests may distrust unknown links, and web pages do not autoplay in chat the way a video does. Ads should show the actual guest experience on a phone.

### Gaps
- I found no data on guest-side preference between links and videos, or on how often guests open links, in Egypt.
