# Click-to-Messenger campaign setup — Congrats (79 EGP invites, 150 EGP/day)

Researched Oct 2026. Sources at the bottom. No reliable public cost-per-conversation benchmark for Egypt exists; the numbers below come from general sources or simple math and are labeled that way.

## 1. Adding the 2nd video ad
- Meta's Help Center still says adding an ad "can reset" learning. Jon Loomer's Sept 2025 test, adding 1 ad to a live ad set with 22 ads, did NOT reset learning. Treat that as "maybe": a small ad set (1 ad becoming 2) is more likely to reset.
- Add the new ad to the SAME ad set. Don't duplicate the ad set or make a new campaign, because that splits a tiny budget. Do it in one edit. Don't change the budget, audience or the existing ad at the same time.
- Use "Use existing post" if the first ad has comments or likes, so it keeps its social proof.
- At about $3/day, run 2–4 ads at most. With more, each ad gets almost no spend. Make the ads clearly different ideas (different hook, story or format). Meta's Andromeda system treats near-copies as one ad.
- Turn off a loser only after it has spent about 2–3× your target cost per conversation with no result. Don't judge it after one day.

## 2. Settings
- Objective: Engagement → Messaging apps → Messenger, with performance goal "Maximize number of conversations". Switch to "Leads" or "Purchases through messaging" only when Meta offers it and you get about 25–50 per week. You won't reach that at 79 EGP × 150/day.
- Placements: Advantage+ (automatic). If the ad is vertical 9:16 video, check that it looks right in Feed (4:5 crop). Messenger Stories ads were removed on 27 Aug 2026. Exclude Audience Network if lots of junk chats come in.
- Audience: Egypt, broad or Advantage+ audience. Optional suggestions: engaged, wedding, engagement (خطوبة/فرح). Ages about 20–40, all genders (both brides and grooms order). Target people living in Egypt, not "people recently in".
- Expect about 18% VAT on top of spend unless you give a VAT number, so the real cost is about 177 EGP/day.

## 3. Message template
- Greeting in Egyptian Arabic that gives the price right away, e.g. "أهلاً! دعوة الفرح الديجيتال بـ79 جنيه بس، جاهزة في دقايق 💍".
- 3–4 FAQ quick replies: "عايز أشوف نماذج"، "السعر كام؟"، "إزاي أطلب؟"، "بتوصل إمتى؟". Set an automatic answer for each one that includes the order link.
- Turn on a Messenger instant reply as a backup, and reply as a person within 5 minutes. Slow replies kill sales.
- Put the price in the ad and the greeting. This screens out people who only want to ask "how much?".

## 4. Learning phase and metrics
- Meta says you need about 50 results in 7 days to leave learning (some say 25 now). Budget math: 1,050 EGP/week ÷ 50 means you need under 21 EGP per conversation. Under 42 EGP gets you 25.
- Wait 5–7 days, or about 50 conversations, before judging an ad.
- What to watch:
  - Cost per messaging conversation started
  - Hook rate (3-second views ÷ impressions): good is 25–30% or more, weak is under 20%
  - Hold rate (15-second views ÷ 3-second views): good is 40–50%
  - CTR (link)
  - Conversation-to-sale rate: track it yourself, it's the real KPI
  - Cost per sale: must stay under your margin
- There is no reliable public Egypt benchmark. One global source gives $0.50–5 per conversation.

## 5. Mistakes to avoid
- Optimizing for cheap conversations and never measuring sales.
- Making edits every day, which resets learning.
- Too many ads or ad sets for 150 EGP.
- Slow or bot-only replies.
- No price shown up front.
- Audience Network junk clicks.
- Tight interest targeting.
- Judging after 1–2 days.
- Never marking buyers as purchases in Meta Business Suite or Page Inbox (labels or "mark as paid"), which you need later for purchase optimization and lookalikes.
